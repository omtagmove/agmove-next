# Product scene photographs

These real photographs illustrate the different work settings; they do not document AgMove deployments. They are downloaded and served locally, only inside the desktop 3D Product experience. The source photographs are unretouched; CSS provides cropping, darkening, parallax and scroll-driven crossfades. Wheat-field delivery uses a bottom-aligned CDN crop to retain the road.

All five are free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), including commercial use. License and source pages checked 2026-11-17. Only the optimized WebP files are published.
