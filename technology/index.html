AgMove technology

Built to sense.
Ready to work.

Connected intelligence. Practical fieldwork.

AgMove combines on-board intelligence with a modular electric rover, helping operators plan tasks, follow progress and put the right attachment to work.

Five layers. One system.

Layered autonomy.

  1. Monitor your rovers from any browser: live status, mission progress and battery level in one view, with the operator able to step in at any time.

  2. Sensor fusion brings LiDAR, RealSense cameras and inertial sensing together, so the rover understands rows, obstacles and terrain as conditions change

  3. Designed for unstructured outdoor environments, from vineyard rows to industrial campuses, with rugged electric 4WD mobility.

  4. RTK GNSS for row-level positioning, with precision dependent on signal quality and operating conditions

  5. Electric 4WD, up to 8 hrs per charge*

    (*Runtime varies with load, task and operating conditions.)

Engineered for fieldwork

Built around
real fieldwork.

Rows to follow. Ground that changes. Different jobs to get done. AgMove brings sensing, navigation and a modular electric platform together around the work.

01

Perception

Read the surroundings.

LiDAR, stereo vision and IMU inputs build a picture of the field, helping the rover recognise crop rows, terrain, workers and machinery around it.

02

Positioning & navigation

Follow the planned pass.

RTK GNSS, waypoint planning and row following connect the field map to the rover’s movement, supporting repeatable routes through the work area.

Positioning depends on correction availability, signal quality and operating conditions.

03

Electric & modular

Change the tool.
Keep the platform.

Electric 4WD and on-board control provide a common base for spraying, carrying materials and working with compatible implements.
